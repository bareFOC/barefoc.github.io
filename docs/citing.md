# Citing SimpleFOC

BareFOC is a port of the algorithms and architecture created by Antun Skuric and the SimpleFOC community.

If you use these algorithms in an academic paper, research project, or engineering publication, please cite the foundational SimpleFOC paper published in the *Journal of Open Source Software*:

> **SimpleFOC: A Field Oriented Control (FOC) Library for Controlling Brushless Direct Current (BLDC) and Stepper Motors**  
> A. Skuric, H. S. Bank, R. Unger, O. Williams, D. González-Reyes  
> *Journal of Open Source Software*, 7(74), 4232 (2022).  
> DOI: [10.21105/joss.04232](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.04232)
